## Intellectual property and licences

The source code is released under the **MIT** licence and the guides, in the repository's `content/` folder, under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)** licence. You may reuse them, commercially too, as long as you credit velo-atelier and release your changes to the guides under the same licence.

Brand names mentioned (part makers, shops) belong to their owners. They are mentioned only to describe part compatibility and imply no partnership.

## Liability

The guides are written with care but replace neither the manufacturer's instructions nor a workshop's opinion. Repairing a bike carries risks, especially on brakes, steering and wheels: if in doubt, have the work checked by a professional before riding.

The links to the Rose Bikes, Alltricks and Decathlon shops are plain search links. velo-atelier controls neither their prices, their stock nor their terms of sale.
