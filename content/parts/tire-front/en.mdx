The front tyre carries less weight and often lasts one and a half to two times longer than the rear. Do not ride it to the end: losing grip at the front almost always ends in a crash.

Its size is printed on the sidewall in ETRTO format (for example 28-622). Check the pressure every week: an under-inflated tyre wears fast and punctures more often.
