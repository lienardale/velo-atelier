summary: "The saddle carries your pelvis; its height, setback and tilt matter as much as its shape."
wearSigns:
  - "A torn cover, or padding so compressed you feel the shell."
  - "Bent or cracked rails."
  - "A saddle that tilts or swivels even though the clamp is tight."
---

Pain while riding comes from the setup more often than from the saddle itself: before buying another, check the height, the setback and that the saddle is level.

If you do change it, choose its width from your sit-bone width and riding position: the further forward you lean, the narrower the saddle can be.
