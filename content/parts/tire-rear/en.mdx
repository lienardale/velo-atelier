On the road, a rear tyre usually lasts 2,500 to 5,000 km, much less on a mountain bike on rough ground. A common trick: when the rear is worn, move the front tyre to the rear and fit a new tyre at the front, where grip matters most.

A new tyre must have the same ETRTO diameter as the rim and a width that suits both the rim and the frame clearance.
