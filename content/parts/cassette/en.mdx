A cassette usually outlives two or three chains, as long as each chain is replaced at its wear limit. It is chosen by number of speeds, range (for example 11-34) and freehub type: HG, XD, Micro Spline… which are not interchangeable.

Bikes with 7 speeds or fewer often have a screw-on freewheel rather than a cassette: the part and the removal tool are different.
