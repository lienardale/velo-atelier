summary: "The chain carries the force from the pedals to the rear wheel. It stretches as it wears, and then wears out the cassette and chainrings."
wearSigns:
  - "Stretch of 0.5 % (11–12 speed) or 0.75 % (10 speed or fewer) on a chain checker."
  - "A chain that skips under load, especially on the small sprockets."
  - "Stiff links, rust, or a creak on every pedal stroke."
lifespanKm: 3000
---

A chain lasts on average 2,000 to 5,000 km depending on care, weather and the number of speeds: the more speeds, the narrower the chain and the faster it wears. Regular cleaning and lubrication often doubles its life.

Measure its stretch every time you clean it. Replaced in time, the chain is the only part to change; worn too far, it takes the cassette and sometimes the chainrings with it.
