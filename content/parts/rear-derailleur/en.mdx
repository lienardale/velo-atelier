---
partId: rear-derailleur
summary: "The mechanism that moves the chain from one sprocket to the next. It needs adjusting far more often than replacing."
wearSigns:
  - "Jockey wheels with pointed teeth, or that wobble as they turn."
  - "A derailleur leaning towards the spokes: a bent hanger."
  - "Play in the pivots, or a spring that no longer keeps the chain taut."
---

Most shifting problems come from the cable, the housing or the derailleur hanger, not from the derailleur itself. Before replacing it, check the hanger alignment and adjust the indexing.

A replacement derailleur must match the number of speeds, the brand of the shifters and the size of the largest sprocket on the cassette.
