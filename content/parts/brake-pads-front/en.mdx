Disc brake pads and rim brake pads are the parts that wear fastest on a bike. How long they last depends enormously on terrain and weather: a few hundred kilometres in mud and long descents, several thousand on dry roads.

Check them every month and before a ride in the mountains. Always replace both pads of the same brake together, and choose the exact model made for your caliper.
