A rotor usually lasts as long as two to four sets of pads. It is bolted to the hub with six bolts or a Center Lock lockring: a replacement must have the same mount and diameter, or the caliper no longer lines up with it.

Never touch it with greasy fingers and clean it only with isopropyl alcohol. A slightly bent rotor can sometimes be straightened, but a rotor that is too thin must be replaced.
