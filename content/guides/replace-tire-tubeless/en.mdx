<Step id="add-sealant">

The cleanest way to add sealant is through the valve. Deflate the tyre fully, turn the wheel so the valve is at the top, then remove the core with the <Tool id="valve-core-tool" alt="pliers" />. Wait a few seconds for any sealant already inside to run down.

Inject the amount given on the bottle with the <Tool id="sealant-injector" />, according to the tyre width: a mountain bike tyre needs far more than a road tyre. Screw the core back in, pump up, then spin and shake the wheel in every direction to spread the liquid over the whole inside surface.

</Step>
