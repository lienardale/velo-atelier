<Step id="mark-position">

Before touching anything, write down the current position: you will want to get back to it. Measure saddle height, from the centre of the bottom bracket axle to the top of the saddle, along the seat tube, with the <Tool id="tape-measure" alt="steel-ruler" />.

Measure the setback too: the horizontal distance between the bottom bracket axle and a plumb line dropped from the nose of the saddle. Finally, note the distance from the saddle nose to the centre of the handlebar. A new saddle has a different shape; these numbers give you a starting point to fine-tune as you ride.

</Step>
