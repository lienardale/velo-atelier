<Step id="wash">

First rinse off the mud with gentle water, never a pressure washer, which drives grease out of the bearings. Then wash the frame from top to bottom with warm water and a little washing-up liquid, using a sponge or a <Tool id="brush" alt="rags" />, and keep a separate sponge for the drivetrain.

Dry with <Tool id="rags" /> and look closely at the welds, the underside of the down tube and the rear dropouts: a hairline crack or cracked paint needs a workshop's opinion.

</Step>
