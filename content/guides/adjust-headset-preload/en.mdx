<Step id="set-preload">

Loosen the stem clamp bolts on the steerer with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />. Then tighten the top cap bolt a quarter turn at a time, holding the front brake on and rocking the bike back and forth, until the knocking disappears.

Lift the front end: the bar should turn freely, with no tight spot. Line the stem up with the wheel, then tighten its bolts in turn to the printed torque with the <Tool id="torque-wrench" />.

</Step>
