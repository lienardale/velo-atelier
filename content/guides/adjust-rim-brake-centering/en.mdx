<Step id="centering">

Pull the lever and watch which block touches the rim first. On a V-brake or a cantilever, each arm has a small spring tension screw: with the <Tool id="phillips-screwdriver" alt="multi-tool" />, screw it in a quarter turn on the side of the block that sits too close, or out on the other side, until both blocks touch at the same time.

On a road caliper, slightly loosen the mounting nut with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />, centre the caliper by hand and tighten again. Leave 1 to 2 mm between each block and the rim.

</Step>
