<Step id="fore-aft">

Find the bump at the base of your big toe and mark its position on the side of the shoe. Loosen the cleat bolts with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" /> and slide the cleat so its centre sits under that mark, or a few millimetres behind it, measured with a <Tool id="steel-ruler" alt="tape-measure" />.

Set both shoes the same way, tighten firmly, then check the angle: your heel must touch neither the crank nor the chainstay.

</Step>
