<Step id="protect-brakes">

Put the bike in a <Tool id="work-stand" /> and tuck a folded rag around each rotor or brake block. Apply <Tool id="degreaser" alt="isopropyl-alcohol" /> to the cassette, chainrings and jockey wheels, leave it for a few minutes, then scrub every part with a <Tool id="brush" alt="rags" /> while turning the pedals backwards.

Rinse with clean water, never a pressure washer, dry with <Tool id="rags" />, then lube the chain link by link as described in the chain guide.

</Step>
