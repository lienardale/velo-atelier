<Step id="limit-screws">

Put the chain on the small chainring and the largest sprocket. With the <Tool id="phillips-screwdriver" alt="multi-tool" />, set the L screw to leave one millimetre between the chain and the inner cage plate. Then shift onto the big chainring and the smallest sprocket, and set the H screw to leave the same gap on the outside.

Before anything else, check that the cage clears the teeth of the big chainring by one to two millimetres and runs parallel to it. Then re-tension the cable.

</Step>
