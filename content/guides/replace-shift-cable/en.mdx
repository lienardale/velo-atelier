<Step id="remove-old">

Without pedalling, click the shifter all the way to the smallest cog at the back, or to the small chainring at the front: the cable is now slack and its head sits opposite the exit. Undo the bolt that clamps the cable at the derailleur and cut off the end cap with the <Tool id="cable-cutter" />.

Find where the cable leaves the shifter: under a screwed cover, under the hood of a road lever or at the end of a trigger shifter. Push the cable from the derailleur towards the shifter, and pull it out by its head. Note how the housing is routed before removing it.

</Step>
