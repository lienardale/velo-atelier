<Step id="clean-rim">

Dampen <Tool id="rags" /> with <Tool id="isopropyl-alcohol" /> and wipe the braking track on both sides of the rim while turning the wheel, until the rag no longer turns black. Avoid anything greasy, polish or lube.

Then scrub the surface of the brake blocks with a <Tool id="brush" alt="rags" /> and pick out small embedded metal chips with a toothpick: they score the rim. Test the brakes at low speed before riding off.

</Step>
