<Step id="remove-pedals">

The right pedal, on the chain side, unscrews anticlockwise, like an ordinary bolt. The left one has a reverse thread and unscrews clockwise. Remember one simple rule: to undo either pedal, always turn the top of the wrench towards the back of the bike.

Set the crank arm forward, fit the <Tool id="pedal-wrench" alt="allen-keys" /> on the flats of the axle, or an Allen key from behind the crank arm, and push sharply. A seized pedal comes free more easily with a long wrench, while holding the other crank arm.

</Step>
