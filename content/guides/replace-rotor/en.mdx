<Step id="fit-rotor">

Look at the arrow engraved on the new rotor: it shows the direction the wheel turns when riding forwards, and must face outwards. On a six-bolt rotor, set it on the hub, then start all six bolts by hand with the <Tool id="torx-keys" alt="multi-tool" />.

Tighten them in a star pattern, in small steps, to the torque given by the rotor or hub maker, using the <Tool id="torque-wrench" />. A Center Lock rotor is held by a single lockring, tightened with the matching splined tool. Finally, clean the whole braking track with isopropyl alcohol.

</Step>
