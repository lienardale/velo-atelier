<Step id="reach-screw">

Sit on the bike with your hands on the grips: your wrists and the levers should form a straight line. To change the angle, loosen the lever clamp with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />, rotate it around the bar and tighten again.

Then find the small reach screw or dial near the pivot of the blade: screw it in to bring the blade closer to your fingers. After adjusting, the lever must still brake firmly well before it reaches the bar or your other fingers.

</Step>
