<Step id="clamp-cable">

Screw the barrel adjuster on the lever or the brake almost fully in, then back it out one turn: that leaves you adjustment in both directions. Hold the brake arms squeezed against the rim, or the mechanical caliper's arm closed, and pull the cable tight with the pliers to take up the slack.

Tighten the cable clamp bolt with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />, firmly but without crushing the strands. Release the brake, then set the gap of the blocks or pads with the barrel adjuster: the lever should turn firm well before it reaches the handlebar.

</Step>
