<Step id="clean-rotors">

Dampen <Tool id="rags" /> with <Tool id="isopropyl-alcohol" /> and wipe both faces of the rotor while turning the wheel, until the rag comes away clean. Do not touch the braking surface with your fingers again, and never use chain degreaser or polish.

If the brake still squeals, remove the pads with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />, sand their surface on fine sandpaper laid flat, clean them with alcohol, refit them and bed them in.

</Step>
