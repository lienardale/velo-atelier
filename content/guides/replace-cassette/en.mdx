<Step id="remove-lockring">

Lay the wheel flat, cassette side up, and remove the quick-release skewer. Fit the <Tool id="cassette-lockring-tool" /> into the splines of the lockring and put the <Tool id="adjustable-wrench" alt="pliers" /> on it. Wrap the chain of the <Tool id="chain-whip" /> around a large cog, in the direction that stops the cassette turning anticlockwise.

Hold the whip firmly and push the wrench anticlockwise: the lockring lets go with a loud crack, which is normal. Remove it, then slide the cogs off the freehub body, keeping their spacers in order.

</Step>
