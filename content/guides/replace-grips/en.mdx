<Step id="remove-grips">

Lock-on grips come off easily: undo the small bolt on each clamp with the <Tool id="allen-keys" alt="multi-tool" />, then twist the grip off. For plain push-on grips, slide the blade of a <Tool id="flat-screwdriver" alt="multi-tool" /> under the inner edge, without scratching the bar.

Pour a little isopropyl alcohol into the gap and twist the grip: it slides off. The alcohol evaporates within minutes without leaving a greasy film, and can also help you slide the new grips on. Remove the shifters first if they are in the way.

</Step>
