<Step id="wash-gently">

With the battery out and the system off, wash the bike with a sponge and warm soapy water, or a gentle hose. Never aim water at the motor, the display, the connectors or the battery housing, and never use a pressure washer, which forces water past the seals.

Clean the battery contacts dry, with a small paintbrush or a <Tool id="brush" alt="rags" />, then dry the whole bike with <Tool id="rags" /> before refitting the battery.

</Step>
